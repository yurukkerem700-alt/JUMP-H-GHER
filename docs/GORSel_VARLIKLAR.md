# Görsel yönü ve üretilen varlıklar

Yeni raster varlıklar yerleşik imagegen aracıyla üretildi. Dosyalar proje içinde saklanır; dış bir görsel URL'sine bağımlı değildir.

- `Assets/CarGameArt/GameIcon.png`: Kök Gezgin uygulama ikonu; ana menü ve web açılışında da kullanılır.
- `Assets/CarGameArt/ThemeAtlas.png`: dört bağımsız üst bölge arka planı, 2×2 atlas. `AltitudeWorld` hücreleri sprite olarak ayırır.
- `Assets/CarGameArt/RootWandererSheet.png`: özgün karakterin sekiz pozlu sayfası.
- `Assets/CarGameArt/MutantRavenSheet.png`: dört kanat pozlu kuş sayfası.
- `Assets/CarGameArt/CloudSky.png`: ilk yükseliş geçişindeki bulut katmanı.

## İkon için kullanılan son prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: square native game application icon for an original dark 2D upward platform game Kök Yankısı. No text or letters. Bold close portrait of a tiny mysterious hooded root wanderer: weathered ivory wooden mask, two glowing amber eyes, charcoal brown hood, short crimson scarf, a few glowing red roots curling around lower edge. Background deep violet storm clouds and subtle warm amber halo behind head. Beautiful hand-painted 2D indie game art, earthy colors, readable expressive silhouette at tiny sizes, centered symmetrical face occupies 65% of square. Full bleed square, no rounded-corner border, no UI, no photorealism, no skull gore. This is a new original mascot icon.

## Bölge atlası için kullanılan son prompt

Asset type: production 2D platform game background atlas. One 2048x1152 landscape image subdivided precisely into a 2 by 2 grid of four equally sized 1024x576 wide landscape background paintings, no borders or gutters. Each panel is an independent full background with NO character, platforms, coins, text, labels, interface. Hand-painted dark fantasy indie game environment, richly layered silhouettes with atmospheric perspective, restrained details and unobstructed middle gameplay area. TOP LEFT: immense drifting violet clouds with warm amber distant horizon, serene eerie high sky. TOP RIGHT: dark blue thunderstorm clouds around distant broken gothic floating towers, pale lightning far behind, storm citadel. BOTTOM LEFT: deep crimson suspended cavern of ancient twisted roots around edges, glowing red veins, warm earthy fog, floating distant rock silhouettes. BOTTOM RIGHT: indigo cosmic upper sky, sparse luminous stars and distant shattered moon fragments, purple astral mist and small floating ruins. Palette low saturation and dark enough for foreground readability. Four panels have distinctly different architecture and atmosphere. Full bleed landscapes, no ground/platform foreground, no panel frames.
